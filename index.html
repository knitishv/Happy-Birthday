To My Love

In a fine morning of 10th August, you were at home and I was in night shift. When I came back from office in the morning, I was thinking about you. I was imagining you sleeping and I couldn't stop smiling. It was like, I was constantly looking at you while you are in deep sleep, how cute you looked 🥰. You looked like a pretty little baby girl 😍. I were so deep into thinking about you that I couldn't stop myself from writing a small piece of poetry for you while you are away 💖.

How do I describe your beauty, I don't know.
How do I express my love, I don't know.
How do I convey your importance in my life, my life.
The world feels like passing by me when I am alone, but how do I tell the same world feels alive when you are beside me.
I could do this, I could do that, I could do a lot of things; but how do I tell you all my deed feels worthless without you, I don't know.
